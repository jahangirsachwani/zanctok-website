01 · cGMP Compliance

Current Good Manufacturing Practices, applied everywhere

Our health care products are a reflection of our pre-set safety and quality standards bound by stringent regulatory guidelines. Our manufacturing processes are performed under qualified supervision, ensuring efficiency and productivity at all levels.

We apply internationally recognised Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP) to ensure quality and safety. cGMP covers all aspects of manufacturing — standard operating procedures, proficient equipment, accurate instrumentation and competent technical staff — allowing us to fulfil market demand on time while using our facility to its maximum capacity.

02 · Quality Operations

A production facility designed for quality

Our state-of-the-art production facility, located at Hyderabad's major industrial hub, is designed to facilitate the production of quality products. We adhere strictly to the principles of cGMP with modern, up-to-date production facilities, efficient production control, quality assurance, industrial safety, occupational health and environmental control systems.

Raw materials are sourced from globally renowned manufacturers and subjected to a demanding schedule of testing procedures. Production at the facility is approved through regular inspections by local regulatory authorities and by the principal companies whose products are manufactured under licence by Zanctok.

03 · Quality Control

A fully equipped laboratory

We have a completely equipped laboratory to check and ensure the quality of every medicine. Our experienced staff work with advanced instruments and thorough documentation, following recognised good laboratory practice standards.

Our manufacturing plant has the latest analytical instruments, and we monitor quality through a Quality Management System (QMS) that ensures the consistent quality of our products. The growing demand for our products reflects the confidence of doctors and patients in their quality.

04 · Quality Assurance

Confidence built into every product

Our well-established Quality Assurance Department sets the requirements for developing and manufacturing reliable products. It ensures customer confidence and company credibility while improving work processes and efficiency.

Our QA specialists develop quality standards, conduct tests and identify issues in production and services — ultimately ensuring that every final product meets our company standards across the board.

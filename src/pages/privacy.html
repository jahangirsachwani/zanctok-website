Privacy Policy

Zanctok Pharmaceutical Laboratories ("ZPL", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry forms — your name, organisation, email, phone number, city and message when you contact us or send a partnership enquiry.
  • Side-effect reports — reporter contact details and limited patient information (initials, age, sex, weight) together with details of the medicine and reaction.
  • Technical data — standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by our hosting provider for security.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or partnership request.
  • To assess and follow up side-effect reports and, where required by law, share them with the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP).
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Retention & security

Information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by regulation. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing zanctokpharma@hotmail.com. Some pharmacovigilance records must be retained by law.

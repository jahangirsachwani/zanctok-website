Who we are

A leading pharmaceutical company, built on quality

Zanctok Pharmaceutical Laboratories was incorporated on 21st March 1984 in Hyderabad, Pakistan.

We are dedicated to developing, manufacturing and delivering high-quality medicines that improve patients' lives. With a strong focus on innovation, quality and customer satisfaction, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner in the pharmaceutical industry.

Today, our state-of-the-art plant at S.I.T.E. Hyderabad produces more than 138 registered products — tablets, capsules, syrups, suspensions, ointments and cephalosporins — supplied to over 100 cities nationwide.

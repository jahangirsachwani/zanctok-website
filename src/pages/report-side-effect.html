Pharmacovigilance

Report a suspected side effect

Patient safety is our highest priority. If you or someone you care for has experienced a side effect with a Zanctok product, please let us know. All reports are treated confidentially.

In a medical emergency, contact your doctor or go to the nearest hospital immediately. This form is not monitored around the clock.
Why reporting matters

Every report helps make medicines safer

Side-effect reports help us and the regulatory authorities continuously monitor the safety of medicines. You don't need to be certain the medicine caused the reaction — a suspicion is enough to report.

Healthcare professionals and patients can also report directly to the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP) through its pharmacovigilance reporting channels.

  • Reports are handled confidentially by our safety team
  • We may contact you for follow-up information
  • You can also call us on +92 331 2266602

Our plant

Modern, meticulous and built for quality

Operations at Zanctok Pharmaceutical Laboratories are based on the most modern and meticulous techniques. Our facility is constructed to control cross-contamination, ensure a smooth flow of personnel and materials, provide an optimum environment and assure worker welfare and safety.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is a depiction of our progressive vision, producing solid and liquid oral dosage forms — tablets, capsules, syrups and suspensions — as well as topical preparations.
